# PdfInfo

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Viewing`

rc.29 (DES-22 §3.1): a PDF as the viewer found it - its pages' sizes as shown, its outline and notices.

## Members

- **Notices** *(property)* - Notices to show above the pages ().
- **Outline** *(property)* - The outline (bookmarks), flattened with their depth.
- **PageCount** *(property)* - The pages the file has; more than when it has more than are shown (2,000).
- **Pages** *(property)* - The pages, each with its size as shown (after its turn and crop), in points.
