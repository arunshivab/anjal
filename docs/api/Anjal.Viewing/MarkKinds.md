# MarkKinds

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Viewing`

The kinds of mark (§7.4, §7.5).

## Members

- **All** *(field)* - Every kind.
- **Arrow** *(field)* - An arrow.
- **Ellipse** *(field)* - An ellipse.
- **Highlight** *(field)* - A highlight: a filled box at 35% opacity.
- **Line** *(field)* - A straight line.
- **Pen** *(field)* - The freehand pen.
- **Picture** *(field)* - A picture the person added (JPEG or PNG), or their own scanned signature.
- **Rectangle** *(field)* - A rectangle.
- **Signature** *(field)* - A signature drawn on the pad, kept as strokes.
- **Text** *(field)* - A text box.
