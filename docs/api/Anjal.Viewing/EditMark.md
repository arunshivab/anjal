# EditMark

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Viewing`

One mark on a page of the result (§7.4, §7.5).

## Members

- **Color** *(property)* - Its colour, "#rrggbb".
- **H** *(property)* - Its box's height, in points.
- **Kind** *(property)* - What it is ().
- **Line** *(property)* - The line's width, in points.
- **Page** *(property)* - The page of the result (0-based).
- **Picture** *(property)* - For a picture: which of the request's pictures (0 is the first after the PDFs).
- **Points** *(property)* - For a line, an arrow, the pen and a drawn signature: the points, as x and y in turn.
- **Size** *(property)* - For a text box: the size of its letters, in points.
- **Strokes** *(property)* - For a drawn signature: where each stroke starts in (as a point's number).
- **Text** *(property)* - For a text box: its words.
- **W** *(property)* - Its box's width, in points.
- **X** *(property)* - The left of its box, in points.
- **Y** *(property)* - The top of its box, in points.
