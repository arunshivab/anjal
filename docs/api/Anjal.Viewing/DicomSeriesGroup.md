# DicomSeriesGroup

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Viewing`

rc.30: one series and its slices, in the toolkit's order.

## Members

- **Description** *(property)* - The series description (display-safe).
- **Entries** *(property)* - The ZIP entry of each slice, in order (the archive's places, as the archive viewer names them).
- **Frames** *(property)* - The frames in all: a multi-frame file counts its frames.
- **Modality** *(property)* - The modality (display-safe).
- **Number** *(property)* - The series' number in the whole grouping, from 1 (the address of its series view).
- **Slices** *(property)* - The files of the series.
