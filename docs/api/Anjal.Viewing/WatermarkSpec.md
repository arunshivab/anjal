# WatermarkSpec

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Viewing`

A watermark drawn across every page (§7.7, D-376).

## Members

- **Angle** *(property)* - Its angle, anticlockwise, in degrees.
- **Color** *(property)* - Its colour, "#rrggbb".
- **Opacity** *(property)* - How strongly it shows, from 0.05 to 1.
- **Size** *(property)* - The size of its letters, in points.
- **Text** *(property)* - Its words.
