# MailboxActivity

**Namespace:** `Anjal.Store`

What a mailbox did over a period. Everything the dashboard shows.

## Members

- **ByCategory** *(property)* - Received messages per category, including "Uncategorised".
- **ByDay** *(property)* - Sent and received per day, oldest first.
- **ByFolder** *(property)* - Received messages per folder.
- **Delivered** *(property)* - Messages delivered to a folder other than Junk.
- **From** *(property)* - Start of the period (inclusive, UTC).
- **Junked** *(property)* - Messages filed in Junk.
- **ReceivedWithAttachments** *(property)* - Messages received carrying at least one attachment.
- **RecoveredFromJunk** *(property)* - Junk messages the reader moved back to INBOX.
- **Sent** *(property)* - Messages sent.
- **SentWithAttachments** *(property)* - Messages sent carrying at least one attachment.
- **To** *(property)* - End of the period (exclusive, UTC).
- **TopSenders** *(property)* - The five people who wrote most.
